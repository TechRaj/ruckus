Privacy policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Ruckus is an app for saving places with a small group of friends, called a Den. This page says what Ruckus collects, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it.

Who we are

Ruckus is made by Karthikraj Sivakumar, Shruthi Murali, Amelia Lon and Quan Teng Wai, a team based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("we"). Karthikraj Sivakumar publishes Ruckus on the App Store and is responsible for your personal information. He is our privacy officer (also called the data protection officer). You can reach him at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

The short version: we keep what you need to use the app with your friends: your email, the places you save and what you write about them. We don't sell data, show ads based on it, or track you across other apps. You can delete everything from inside the app.

What we use but don't keep

  • Your location, if you allow it. The app sends it with a request to sort places by distance. It is used for that answer and not stored, and your Den never sees it.
  • The text of a post you share. When you share a link, your phone reads the post's public preview (the same title, caption and image a messaging app shows). The caption is sent to our server and on to an AI model to find the places it names, then thrown away. We don't log it or keep it. We never download or store the video or image.
  • Place names you search for, which are looked up with Google Maps.
  • A sign-in code that didn't work. The email and code are checked once more by our server (in case it's the App Review demo account), then discarded. They are not logged.

These services may store and process data outside Canada and Singapore, including in the United States, where the law may give it different protection. We only use services that are contractually bound to protect it and to use it only to provide their service to us.

Who can see what

Places, notes, comments, votes and plans in a Den are visible to that Den's members and no one else. Your name and avatar are visible to people who share a Den with you. Your email is not visible to anyone.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or your account. A report is kept for as long as it takes to deal with, and no more than a year after that.

Deleting your account

In the app: People → Delete account. It happens straight away, and it cannot be undone. It removes your email, profile, saves, comments, votes and memberships from every Den. A place a friend also saved stays in the Den under their name. A Den you own passes to the next member. Records of which suggestion you picked, and reports, stay with your account ID removed. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Ruckus Pro subscription. Cancel that in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Consent

By creating an account you consent to the uses on this page. Location and notifications are optional and only used if you allow them in iOS. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning those off or by deleting your account. If you do, the parts of the app that need them stop working.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your privacy regulator: in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information); in Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Commission; in the EU or UK, your local data protection authority.

Keeping it safe

Data is encrypted in transit, and each Den's data is only readable by its members. This is enforced by the database, not just the app. If a breach puts your information at real risk, we'll tell you and the relevant regulators as the law requires.

Children

Ruckus is not for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
